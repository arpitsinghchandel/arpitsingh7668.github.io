1. What this covers

This policy explains what happens to your information when you visit this portfolio website. This is a small personal site — it doesn't run ads, doesn't use analytics or tracking cookies, and doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.

2. Information collected through the contact form

If you use the contact form, the name, email address, and message you enter are submitted to Formspree, a third-party form-handling service, which forwards the message by email. That's the only personal information this Site actively collects, and it's only collected if you choose to submit the form.

Formspree processes that data on its own servers under its own privacy policy. You can review it directly at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.
